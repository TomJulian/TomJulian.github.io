collaborations

How I work with academic and industry partners.

The most useful collaborations connect a clinically important question with the methods, data, and expertise needed to answer it well. I work across disciplinary boundaries and am particularly interested in partnerships that can move from biological discovery to credible clinical evaluation.

Clinical imaging and cohorts

I collaborate with clinicians and cohort researchers to develop imaging phenotypes and study ophthalmic images collected in prospective studies or routine care.

Genetics and multi-omic discovery

I work with academics in statistical genetics, metabolomics, causal inference, and biological interpretation to investigate the mechanisms underlying eye disease and imaging-derived traits.

AI methods and validation

I am interested in collaborations around representation learning, multimodal modelling, external validation, and prospective evaluation, with clinical relevance shaping the technical question from the outset.

Clinical translation and industry

I welcome partnerships focused on clinically meaningful use cases, robust evaluation, and potential integration into care pathways. My role is to help frame and evaluate research, rather than to act as a route to data access.

Supervision and academic community

I am happy to discuss research supervision, collaborative grants, speaking, and opportunities to connect researchers across ophthalmology, AI, genetics, and population health.
