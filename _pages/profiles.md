The most useful collaborations connect a clinically important question with the methods, data, and expertise needed to answer it well. I work across disciplinary boundaries and am particularly interested in partnerships that can move from biological discovery to credible clinical evaluation.

<div class="collaboration-grid">
  <section>
    <h2>Clinical imaging and cohorts</h2>
    <p>I collaborate with clinicians and cohort researchers to develop imaging phenotypes and study ophthalmic images collected in prospective studies or routine care.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>Genetics and multi-omic discovery</h2>
    <p>I work with academics in statistical genetics, metabolomics, causal inference, and biological interpretation to investigate the mechanisms underlying eye disease and imaging-derived traits.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>AI methods and validation</h2>
    <p>I am interested in collaborations around representation learning, multimodal modelling, external validation, and prospective evaluation, with clinical relevance shaping the technical question from the outset.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>Clinical translation and industry</h2>
    <p>I welcome partnerships focused on clinically meaningful use cases, robust evaluation, and potential integration into care pathways. My role is to help frame and evaluate research, rather than to act as a route to data access.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>Supervision and academic community</h2>
    <p>I am happy to discuss research supervision, collaborative grants, speaking, and opportunities to connect researchers across ophthalmology, AI, genetics, and population health.</p>
  </section>
</div>
