I use genetic, metabolomic, and other omics datasets to investigate the biological processes underlying imaging-derived phenotypes and ophthalmic and systemic disease.

A central aim of this work is to move beyond statistical association: identifying potential causal relationships, clarifying disease mechanisms, and finding biological pathways that may support future therapeutic or diagnostic research.

Our genome-wide association studies have explored the genetic determinants of retinal layer thickness{% cite julian2025pigmentation %}, foveal morphology{% cite green2025genetic %}, and deep learning-derived ophthalmic imaging features{% cite julian2026multiomic %}.

I have also used causal inference methods to investigate potentially modifiable disease risk factors, including work examining the relationship between strenuous exercise and motor neuron disease{% cite julian2021physical obrien2025extreme %}.
